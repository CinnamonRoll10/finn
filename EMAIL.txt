WHAT I BUILT
FINN works in two modes.
Push: a windowed digest of every exchange filing across a 20-stock portfolio, triaged so results, M&A, management changes and litigation surface first and routine paperwork is hidden. Results filings show Revenue, EBITDA, PBT and PAT with QoQ and YoY, read from the results PDF; management changes carry a bio of the incoming person read from the Reg-30 filing. End-of-day price and volume alerts fire against thresholds the user sets.
Pull: on-demand research on any NSE symbol from primary sources only (annual report, the last four concall transcripts and standardised financials). Its centrepiece is a promised-vs-delivered ledger that grades each guidance statement against the quarter it applied to, plus a grounded Q&A that tags every claim with its source.

KEY DECISIONS
- Rules before models: NSE's own filing types plus regexes classify about 95% of filings for free; only the survivors get a model call.
- Two model tiers routed by job: a cheap model reads every document once (cached forever on content), a strong model synthesises once per company and answers questions. A full research build costs about $0.07 in API terms, and the app exposes its own usage ledger.
- Numbers are computed in Python, never generated. The strong model may not do arithmetic or use outside knowledge, and must tag every bullet with its source.
- Instant digest, background detail: triage returns in milliseconds and enriches while you read.

LEFT OUT, DELIBERATELY: intraday prices and native push notifications, BSE filings (their API blocks scripts), news and broker research, auth and multi-user.
NEXT: guidance as a first-class tracked object with "promise missed" alerts, a results-day diff of this call against the last, batch-API warming, and a voice front-end over the grounded Q&A.
